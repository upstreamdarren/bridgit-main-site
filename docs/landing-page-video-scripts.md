# Bridgit landing-page video series

## Shared production format

- **Audience:** commissioners, service leaders, partnership leads and social-impact decision-makers
- **Length:** 70–75 seconds
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape, composed centrally so key shots can also be cropped to 9:16
- **Structure:** problem → connected need → Bridgit solution → human handover and organisational value → CTA
- **Narration:** warm, confident British voice; conversational rather than corporate; approximately 135–145 words per minute
- **Visual language:** polished editorial motion illustration matching the Bridgit website; rounded cards, layered paper-like textures, natural and inclusive characters, subtle grain and gentle parallax
- **Brand palette:** ink `#172033`, purple `#6424ba`, deep purple `#351066`, pink `#d63ab7`, blue `#278fca`, teal `#168f91` and soft mist `#f6f3fb`
- **Typography added in post:** Nunito or a close rounded sans serif
- **Transitions:** connected lines, flowing pathways and cards that reorganise from confusion into one clear journey
- **Generation workflow:** treat each numbered scene as a separate 6–10 second clip, preserve the same lead character and visual seed across its video, then assemble the clips under the continuous talkover
- **Important:** generate clean scenes without embedded text. Add all headings, labels, statistics, logo and CTA text during editing for accuracy.
- **Common end card:** Bridgit logo, “Extend human support. Reach people earlier.” and “Book a 30-minute discovery call at bridgit.care”.

## 1. Adult carers

### Talkover script

**Problem**

Every community has unpaid carers your service has not met yet. They are supporting a parent, partner, child or friend—but many do not recognise themselves as a carer. They search for help late at night, when work, money, appointments and worry have already started to pile up. Too often, they reach a real person only when they are close to breaking point.

**Solution**

Bridgit gives your carer service an always-available digital extension. Friendly coaches use your approved guidance and local pathways to help people recognise their caring role, understand their options and turn advice into a practical personal plan. Check-ins keep them moving, and when human judgement or deeper support is needed, a warm handover brings your team in—with the carer’s consent and the right context.

This is not about replacing trusted staff. It is about reaching hidden carers earlier, answering routine questions consistently and protecting human time for relationships, safeguarding and complex need.

**CTA**

If you want to reach more carers before crisis, book a discovery call with Bridgit.

## 2. Local support

### Talkover script

**Problem**

Residents experience one life—not a set of council departments. A housing problem can quickly connect to debt, caring, health and isolation. But support is often spread across different teams, websites and community organisations. People do not know where to begin, repeat their story at every front door and wait until a manageable problem becomes urgent.

**Solution**

Bridgit creates one approachable starting point for early help. Residents can begin by phone, web, WhatsApp or a trusted community link. Digital coaches use approved council information and neighbourhood pathways to understand connected needs, explain useful next steps and guide people to the right council, VCSE or community support.

When a professional is needed, a consented warm handover carries the relevant context forward. Meanwhile, teams gain a clearer view of emerging demand, the pathways people use and where local gaps are growing.

Bridgit handles navigation and routine guidance so real people can focus on risk, judgement and those who need them most.

**CTA**

Bring us one service problem worth solving. Book a discovery call with Bridgit.

## 3. Social enterprises

### Talkover script

**Problem**

Social enterprises are often closest to the people they serve—but furthest from spare capacity. Your best knowledge may sit with a handful of experienced colleagues. Every new referral competes with delivery, follow-up, funding applications and impact reporting. Growing one-to-one support can mean growing cost just as quickly, while funders increasingly expect clearer evidence of outcomes and social value.

**Solution**

Bridgit helps turn your trusted service model into a digital team. Coaches are shaped around your knowledge, tone, pathways, safeguards and human handover rules. They can support people through web, voice, WhatsApp or a fuller coaching app—providing personalised guidance, practical plans and check-ins between human sessions.

Your specialists remain in control and step in when relationships, risk or judgement matter. At the same time, each journey creates useful evidence about need, engagement, actions and outcomes.

That means greater reach without diluting your purpose, more time for high-value human work and stronger evidence for bids, commissioners and partners.

**CTA**

Bring us the service model you want to scale. Book a discovery call with Bridgit.
