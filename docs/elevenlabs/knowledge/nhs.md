# Bridgit for NHS partners
Bridgit extends approved, preventative and non-clinical support beyond appointments. Digital coaches can explain trusted information in plain language, help people act on practical next steps, connect them to community and voluntary-sector pathways, check in and make agreed human handovers.

It complements clinical and community teams and does not diagnose, prescribe or replace professional judgement. Potential value includes better preparation, continuity between contacts, reduced repeat navigation and insight into non-clinical needs, actions, pathway use and gaps. Governance, content approval, escalation, reporting and integrations are co-designed for the deployment.

Sales and booking: https://bridgit.care/nhs and https://bridgit.care/nhs#book-a-call.
