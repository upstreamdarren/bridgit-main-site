# Bridgit for employers
Bridgit gives employees private, practical guidance around caring, wellbeing, money and work before pressures become absence, burnout or exit. It can help people understand options, prepare for manager conversations, build plans and reach human support when needed.

The model complements people teams, managers, EAP, occupational health and benefits. It can reduce repeat navigation, support carer-friendly practice and provide proportionate, anonymised insight into themes and pathway use. Exact reporting and integrations depend on agreed governance and scope.
