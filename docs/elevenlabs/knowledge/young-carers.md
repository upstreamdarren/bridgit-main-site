# Bridgit for young-carer services
Young carers may not identify with the term, may hide pressures, and often need help outside office hours. Bridgit can provide age-appropriate, approved guidance about school, home life, wellbeing and caring; turn it into manageable steps; and make agreed, consented handovers to trusted adults and services.

The offer extends rather than replaces youth workers, school staff and safeguarding leads. It can support earlier identification, consistent information, check-ins and insight into recurring needs and pathway gaps. Access can include web, voice, messaging, hosted links and partner routes, depending on scope.
