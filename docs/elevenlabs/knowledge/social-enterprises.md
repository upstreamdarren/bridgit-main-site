# Bridgit for Social Enterprises — approved sales knowledge

## The offer

Bridgit helps social enterprises turn trusted knowledge and service models into digital coaches. The aim is to reach more people, provide personalised next steps, continue support between human sessions and turn delivery into useful evidence of need, activity and outcomes.

Bridgit is itself a UK social enterprise and an accredited Social Enterprise UK member. It understands the tension between growing reach, protecting quality, winning work and proving social value.

Digital coaches extend the people behind a mission. They are not intended to replace practitioners, lived-experience leadership or trusted relationships.

## Problems social enterprises are trying to solve

- Demand can grow faster than one-to-one staff capacity.
- Valuable knowledge is often held by a small number of experienced colleagues.
- People need guidance and follow-up between appointments and outside office hours.
- Funders and commissioners increasingly expect evidence of reach, outcomes, learning and social value.
- Growth into new places can risk inconsistency or dilute a carefully developed service model.

## How the model works

1. Choose the expertise to scale: begin with a suitable role from Bridgit's library of around 60 coaches or design a bespoke coach around a focused service problem.
2. Train it in the organisation's approach: use approved knowledge, tone, lived experience, pathways, boundaries, safeguards and human handover rules.
3. Meet people through appropriate channels: web, voice, WhatsApp, hosted links, QR codes or Bridgit's fuller desktop and mobile advice-and-coaching experience.
4. See and improve impact: understand needs, engagement, actions, check-ins, referrals, feedback and outcomes.

Exact channels, integrations, governance and measurement are agreed for each deployment.

## Value for a social enterprise

- Support more people without making growth depend entirely on additional one-to-one capacity.
- Give people a consistent starting point based on approved guidance and organisational values.
- Extend a proven service model into new places and channels.
- Reduce repetitive guidance and routine follow-up so specialists focus on human work.
- Strengthen bids, reporting, partnership discussions and service improvement with better evidence.
- Begin with a focused use case and build from demonstrated value.

## Bridgit's experience

Bridgit began by making support easier to find for unpaid carers. The connected coach model now supports multiple communities and service challenges. Bridgit reports more than 200,000 people supported across 40 local-authority areas.

This experience demonstrates one possible growth journey; technology does not guarantee impact or contract growth by itself. Success depends on the service model, trusted content, delivery partnership, adoption and measurement.
