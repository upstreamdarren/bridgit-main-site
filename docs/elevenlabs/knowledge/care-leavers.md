# Bridgit for leaving-care services
Bridgit gives care-experienced young adults one steady place for approved guidance across housing, money, health, education, employment and daily life between appointments and outside office hours. It connects pressures, creates manageable actions, follows up and can pass relevant context with consent when an adviser should join.

It strengthens rather than replaces personal advisers. Teams can improve continuity, preparation and access to entitlements and local partners while seeing recurring needs, actions, engagement and pathway gaps.

Sales: https://bridgit.care/care-leavers. Support: https://next.bridgit.care/. Booking: https://bridgit.care/care-leavers#book-a-call. Scope, governance, integrations, pricing and timing are confirmed with the team.
