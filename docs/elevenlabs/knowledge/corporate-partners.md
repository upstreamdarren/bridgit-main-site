# Bridgit for corporate partners
Corporate partners can use Bridgit in two ways. First, deploy digital coaches to support their own people around caring and life pressures while complementing human teams. Second, fund social enterprises to turn proven expertise into scalable digital support, widening access and creating measurable social impact.

A partnership can start with one priority, train coaches on approved content and safeguards, deploy through suitable channels, and track reach, needs, actions, engagement and handovers. Bridgit is a UK social enterprise and a Social Enterprise UK member. Claims, reporting measures and partner roles are agreed for each programme.

Sales and booking: https://bridgit.care/corporate-partners and https://bridgit.care/corporate-partners#book-a-call. Commercial terms, delivery scope and impact framework must be confirmed with the team.
