# Bridgit Care — approved main website knowledge

Bridgit turns an organisation's trusted knowledge, services, local pathways, tone, safeguards and handover rules into digital coaches. Coaches give people earlier guidance, practical next steps and ongoing check-ins while real teams retain relationships, judgement, safeguarding and complex work.

Organisations can choose from a library of 60 coaches, train them on approved local content, deploy through web, voice, WhatsApp, hosted links, QR codes or the fuller advice-and-coaching app, and see impact through needs, actions, check-ins, feedback and handovers. Exact channels, integrations and scope are agreed for each deployment.

Bridgit supports adult carers, young carers, employers, council and neighbourhood front doors, care leavers, healthy ageing, social enterprises, NHS partners and corporate social-impact partners. It reports more than 200,000 people supported across 40 UK local-authority areas. These figures and examples do not guarantee identical results.

The model is designed to reach people earlier, reduce repetitive navigation and routine admin, create consented warm handovers, and reveal what people are trying to solve, which pathways they use, where routes fail and where unmet need is emerging.

Routes: https://bridgit.care/ and booking https://bridgit.care/#book-a-call. Pricing, governance, integrations, implementation and final scope must be confirmed with the Bridgit team.
