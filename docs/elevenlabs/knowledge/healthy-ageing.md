# Bridgit for healthy-ageing services
Small pressures involving transport, confidence, money, caring, mobility and loneliness can combine and undermine independence. Bridgit offers a simple voice, web or messaging starting point, connects everyday needs, creates realistic actions and links people to approved local activities, services and human support.

The model supports earlier prevention, accessible entry points, stronger community connection and joined-up guidance. It handles routine navigation while involving professionals for risk, assessment and relationships. Insight can show emerging needs, access barriers, pathway use and gaps.
