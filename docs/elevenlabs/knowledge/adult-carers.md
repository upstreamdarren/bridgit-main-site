# Bridgit for Adult Carer Services — approved sales knowledge

## The offer

Bridgit helps carer services reach more unpaid carers earlier. Digital coaches provide personalised, approved guidance around the clock, connect people to local help, create practical next steps and continue supporting people through plans and check-ins. When judgement, risk or more complex help is needed, the service can make a consented warm handover to the real carer-service team.

Bridgit is designed to extend human teams, not replace them. It can reduce repetitive first-line questions and routine administration so colleagues have more time for relationships, safeguarding, complex decisions and carers with the greatest needs.

## Problems carer services are trying to solve

- Many people support a parent, partner, child, friend or neighbour without identifying as a carer.
- People often reach services only after caring has affected health, money, employment, sleep or relationships.
- Questions and moments of pressure happen outside office hours.
- Information is fragmented and generic directories can be difficult to act on.
- Services need to evidence reach, needs, referrals, engagement and outcomes to commissioners and funders.

## How the model works

1. Identify and welcome: use friendly questions, campaigns, QR codes, hosted links and trusted partner routes to help people recognise their caring role.
2. Guide personally: give relevant information across wellbeing, money, assessments, work, family life, caring responsibilities and local support.
3. Stay alongside: turn guidance into manageable plans and use check-ins to help people act.
4. Connect to people: follow agreed escalation and referral routes when a member of the service team should become involved.

Coaches can be available through web, voice, WhatsApp, hosted links, QR codes and Bridgit's fuller desktop and mobile advice-and-coaching experience. Exact channels and integrations depend on the agreed deployment.

## Value for a carer service

- Reach hidden carers earlier.
- Offer trusted guidance outside office hours.
- Reduce repetitive enquiries while preserving human judgement.
- Strengthen local assessment, wellbeing, financial and community pathways.
- Understand common needs, actions, engagement and outcomes.
- Begin with a focused service and expand as value and trust are demonstrated.

## Evidence and partnership

Bridgit works with organisations across the UK and reports more than 200,000 people supported across 60 local-authority areas. This includes regional My UK Life programmes spanning the 14 West Midlands and 18 South East adult social care authorities. Peterborough combined Bridgit with frontline engagement, local radio and accessible videos made with community champions in six languages and British Sign Language. The published case study reports a 10% increase in previously unidentified unpaid carers being supported within a few months.

Bridgit Care and Carers Trust are working with network partners to develop carer-led digital solutions that scale support, demonstrate impact and respond to rising demand.

These examples illustrate delivery patterns; they are not a guarantee of identical results for every service.
